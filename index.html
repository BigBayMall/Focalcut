FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I send you my raw footage?
After purchasing, you'll receive a welcome email with a link to your private portal. Simply paste your Google Drive or Dropbox folder link. We do not require you to upload massive files directly to our website.
Are these monthly subscriptions?
No. All our packages are one-time purchases. You buy a package (e.g., 15 videos), submit your footage as you go, and use your balance. When you run out, purchase another package.
How long do I have to use my video package?
We recommend using your package within 30-45 days to maintain content momentum, but we are flexible. We work with you to ensure you get the full value.
What if I don't like the hook or pacing?
Every package (except Starter) includes revision rounds. If a hook isn't hitting, tell us. We will adjust the pacing or try a different opening. Our goal is your retention.
How do I receive the final videos?
Once edited and approved, we upload the final, platform-ready MP4 files directly to your designated Google Drive folder and notify you via email.
